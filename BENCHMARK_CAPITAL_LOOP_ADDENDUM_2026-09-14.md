# Logistics Boss Benchmark Addendum — Capital / Automation Loop — 2026-09-14

> Historical record created under the former title **LOGISTICS BOSS**. Official current title: **FLOTRA（フロトラ）**.

Status: current design reference for Capital Expansion v1

## Why this addendum exists
The previous benchmark pack correctly identified autonomous logistics, bottleneck readability and automation, but the implementation underused the strongest lesson: the player needs many meaningful ways to reinvest into a system that visibly grows.

## Factorio — capital lesson
Official Factorio material emphasizes building infrastructure, automation, research, transport belts, trains and logistic robots, with progressively more capable transport systems and advanced modules.

Transferable principle for Logistics Boss:
- start with low-capability manual flow
- buy machinery that replaces labor or raises capacity
- let every solved bottleneck expose a new one downstream
- make higher-tier transport visibly different from the previous tier
- preserve player choice over investment order

Do not copy Factorio's desktop-scale construction density. Logistics Boss remains mobile-first and observer-management focused.

## Builderment — mobile factory lesson
Builderment is a useful additional benchmark because its factory/automation concept is designed around touch interaction rather than a keyboard-and-mouse assumption.

Transferable principle:
- large automation depth can still work on mobile if construction choices are chunked and readable
- upgrades should make the factory physically larger and more legible, not only raise hidden stats
- research / investment should unlock new categories of automation over time

For Logistics Boss, this supports staged equipment packages instead of full free-form belt editing in the first production pass.

## shapez 2 / Satisfactory — spectacle lesson
The visible machine network becoming larger and smoother is itself a reward.

Transferable principle:
- equipment should be satisfying to watch after purchase
- automation is a visual progression system
- the facility at late game should look structurally different from the starting depot

## Mobile idle-tycoon pattern — economic escalation lesson
The useful part is the accelerating economic scale: cheap early purchases, then thousands, millions and beyond.

Adopt:
- clear cash escalation
- increasingly expensive equipment categories
- strong next-purchase anticipation

Reject:
- waiting as the primary gameplay
- ad/IAP pressure
- upgrades whose only effect is a number changing

## Revised synthesis for Logistics Boss
The product should combine:

Factorio-style bottleneck chains
+ mobile-friendly staged equipment choices
+ idle-tycoon economic escalation
+ visible autonomous 3D logistics
+ measured ROI after each major investment

The resulting core loop is:

profit → equipment → visible flow change → measured efficiency → larger profit → larger equipment

The player chooses the investment sequence. The game provides diagnostics and measurements, not the answer.
