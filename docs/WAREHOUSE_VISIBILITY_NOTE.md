# Warehouse Visibility Note

The portrait gameplay camera uses an open-top cutaway presentation. Decorative ceiling trusses and roof light bars must not cross the playable floor or obscure workers, racks, parcels, vehicles, or bottleneck-reading cues.

The retained warehouse identity comes from the back wall, structural columns, utility run, dock canopies, equipment, lighting, and industrial materials rather than foreground roof geometry.

This constraint is covered by `godot/tests/visual_readability_smoke.gd`.
