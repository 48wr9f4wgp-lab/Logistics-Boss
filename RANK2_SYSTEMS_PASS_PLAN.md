# Rank 2 Systems Pass — Phase 1

Goal: replace repeated numerical button pressing with structural logistics decisions.

Scope:
- Rank 2 uses structural crew allocation instead of manual 1–5 task priorities.
- Rank 2 has three fixed expansion zones; each zone offers one mutually-exclusive choice.
- Major facility choices change simulation behavior and the 3D warehouse.
- Repeated equipment level-up controls are retired from the management UI; legacy save effects remain supported.
- Research becomes one-time unlocks instead of repeatable levels.
- Bottleneck Director teaches Rank 1, but from Rank 2 onward diagnoses rather than supplying a one-tap answer.
- Rank 2 progress is represented by completed expansion zones, not a misleading MAX label.

Acceptance:
1. Fresh Rank 2 has at least five workers and staffing plans produce different role allocations.
2. Workers at Rank 2 only accept tasks matching their assigned role.
3. Intake, storage, and packing zones each enforce a one-of-two choice.
4. All six facility choices have measurable trade-offs.
5. No manual task-priority +/- controls or repeatable equipment upgrade cards remain in management UI.
6. Save v3 persists staffing and new facility choices while migrating v1/v2.
7. Static QA and progression smoke pass before main moves.
